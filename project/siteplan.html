⚽ World Cup Pal ⚽

1. Site Name

The name of my website is World Cup Pal. I chose this name because the site is about the 2026 World Cup and it is meant to be like a friend or "pal" that helps fans follow the tournament. The name is short and easy to remember and tells people what the site is about.

Possible domain name: worldcuppal.com

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of World Cup Pal is to give soccer fans information about the 2026 World Cup. The site will show the match schedule, the teams that are playing, and the host cities in the USA, Canada, and Mexico. It will also have a sign up form where fans can enter their email to get reminders before games start. The site is just for information and does not sell tickets.

3. Scenarios

These are questions that people who visit my site might ask:

  • When does my favorite team play and what channel can I watch it on?
  • Which host city should I visit to see a game and what is the stadium like?
  • How do I sign up to get a reminder before a match starts?

4. Color Schema

I picked two main colors for my website. I used these colors on this document too.

Black (#000000) - used for the headers, the navigation bar, and the footer.
Gold (#d4af37) - used for the headings, links, and buttons (accent color).

The background of the pages will be white (#ffffff) so the text is easy to read.

5. Typography

I am using two fonts for my website:

Font Where it is used
Georgia Used for the main headings and titles.
Arial Used for the paragraphs and body text.
